Product FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to Settings > Account > Reset Password. You'll receive an email link
that expires after 24 hours.

Q: What plans are available?
A: We offer three plans: Starter (free, 1 user), Pro ($15/month, up to 5
users), and Enterprise (custom pricing, unlimited users with SSO support).

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. Go to Settings > Data > Export. Exports are provided as a CSV or
JSON file and are emailed to you within 15 minutes for most account sizes.

Q: Is there an API?
A: Yes, all Pro and Enterprise plans include API access. Rate limits are
100 requests/minute on Pro and 1000 requests/minute on Enterprise.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access
until the end of your current billing period, and your data is kept for
30 days in case you want to reactivate.
