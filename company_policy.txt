Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10 AM to 3 PM local time, during which employees
should be reachable via Slack or email.

Equipment: The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 stipend for home
office setup (desk, chair, monitor). Requests go through the IT portal.

Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year, increasing to
20 days after 3 years of tenure. Vacation requests should be submitted at
least 2 weeks in advance through the HR system. Unused vacation days roll
over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses under $50 can be self-approved. Expenses over $50 require
manager sign-off before submission. All expense reports must be filed within
30 days of the purchase date, with itemized receipts attached.
